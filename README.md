# HR-Analytics-Dashboard
HR Analytics Dashboard – Employee Attrition Analysis using Power BI
HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
An HR analytics project developed using Power BI to analyze employee attrition, retention, salary, overtime, job roles, departments, and other workforce factors.

## Objectives

Analyze employee attrition patterns.
Identify factors associated with employee turnover.
Compare attrition across departments, job roles, age groups, gender, overtime, and salary levels.
Provide actionable recommendations for improving employee retention.

## Tools Used

Power BI
Excel
DAX

## Key KPIs

Total Employees: 1.48K
Total Attrition: 238
Attrition Rate: 16.08%
Retention Rate: 83.92%
Average Salary: $6.50K
Average Years at Company: 7.01

## Key Insights

Employees aged 18–25 have the highest attrition rate at 35.77%.
Sales Representatives have the highest job-role attrition at 39.29%.
Employees working overtime have a 30.62% attrition rate.
Employees earning below $5K have the highest salary-slab attrition at 21.65%.
The Sales department has the highest departmental attrition at 20.67%.

## Recommendations

Strengthen career development and mentorship for younger employees.
Review overtime and workload management.
Improve retention strategies for Sales employees.
Review compensation for lower-paid employees.
Establish clearer career progression pathways.
